Langit Nusantara (Nusantara Skies) is an original flight simulation and arcade adventure set across the breathtaking procedural islands and volcanic mountains of the Indonesian archipelago. Steer your red-and-white propeller plane through glowing golden target rings, manage throttle and altitude on authentic lit cockpit instruments, complete legs over island runways, and beat the run timer to conquer the skies.

## How to Play
Fly through the golden rings to score points and extend your run timer. Complete legs of 10 rings to land over island runways for major bonuses. Keep your altitude clear of the tropical terrain and mountain ranges, avoid stalling, and navigate across the archipelago before time runs out.

## Controls
- **Steer & Pitch**: Arrow keys or WASD keys.
- **Throttle**: W / S keys (or Up / Down arrows).
- **Boost / Climb & Descend**: Shift / Ctrl keys, or Arrow keys / WASD.
- **Mouse Steering**: Click and drag left/right on the canvas to bank and steer.
- **Pause**: P key.
- **Mute Audio**: M key.
- **Start / Restart**: Enter key (or click on screen).
- **Reset**: R key.

## Tech Note
Langit Nusantara is built with pure vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, and Web Audio API. It consists entirely of plain static files with zero network requests and zero external libraries or dependencies, running directly from `game/index.html`.
